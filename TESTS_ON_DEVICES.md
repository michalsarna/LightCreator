# LightCreator: tests on devices

LightCreator has been tried on these machines and works there. The program is made for many laser engravers and cutters; a machine that is not on the list may work just as well, it has only not been confirmed yet.

| Device | Kind | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Sculpfun S9 | diode laser engraver and cutter | GRBL |

## Add your machine

Does LightCreator run on your laser? Open an [issue](https://github.com/michalsarna/LightCreator/issues/new) with:

* the make and model of the machine, and what it is (for example a diode laser, a CNC mill, a pen plotter);
* the controller and its firmware with the version (for example GRBL 1.1h);
* the version of LightCreator and the system of the computer (Windows, macOS, Linux);
* what you did with it (a job, framing, jogging, the camera…) and whether something went wrong.

It goes on the list once it is confirmed.
